Recommendation · Homepage hero

Composition 1 — Lower-Third, Left

It is the only composition that scores strong on every axis the homepage must satisfy at once: it delivers the full-bleed dusk awe that closes the perception-vs-reality gap, keeps the capability proof directly above the CTA for the procurement buyer, and does both in a single frame — so it serves the couple and the organiser without becoming two heroes.

It is also the art-direction-native composition: the treatment already called for the lower region to stay calm and reserve a place for the promise and the invitation — this is that frame. Its mobile parity is the best of the six (the lower-third stacks straight into the 4:5 crop), and trust sits at the point of decision (the proximity principle).

The stronger alternatives aren't wrong — they're right for other pages: Composition 4 (Split Panel) is the natural Conferences hero (maximum message-match for paid search), and Composition 3 leans Weddings-ceremonial. One system; emphasis by intent. For the homepage, Composition 1 is objectively the strongest.
