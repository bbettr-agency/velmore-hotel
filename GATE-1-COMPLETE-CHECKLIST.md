## PART D — What must be answered before Design can begin (the Critical gate)

Design Phase (Gate 2) starts as soon as **every 🔴 Critical item** is resolved. Consolidated:

1. **Canonical business name** + **canonical domain** (+ DNS access).
2. **True room count**, room types, and **current room rates**.
3. **Confirmed venue capacities** (weddings + conferences).
4. **Wedding package** inclusions + pricing; **conference** day + residential pricing + inclusions.
5. **Spa** confirmed (Allura) with treatment/package outline + pricing.
6. **Restaurant name** (so the Dining page can exist).
7. **TGCSA grading** answer (yes+certificate, or no → no star claim).
8. **Mission, vision, core values, company story** (the voice + About depend on these).
9. **Revenue ranking** of services (drives homepage/nav order).
10. **Logo (vector)** + **photography availability** (drives palette + whether a shoot is needed).
11. **GBP + CRM + booking-engine** confirmation (backend + local SEO).

🟡 Important and 🟢 Nice-to-have items can be collected in parallel and are needed by Build/Launch, not to *start* Design.
