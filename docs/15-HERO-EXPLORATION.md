# 15 — HERO EXPLORATION

**Project:** Velmoré Hotel & Spa · **Phase:** Gate 2 — visual exploration (not design)
**What this is:** 7 genuinely different Hero *directions*, interrogated, then compared, narrowed, and a **primary + backup** recommended. **No wireframes, no mockups, no code** — this is thinking made explicit before we design one.
**Everything here is measured against:** [`BKB`](../knowledge-base/BUSINESS-KNOWLEDGE-BASE.md) v0.2 · Marketing (`docs/05`, `docs/08`) · **Visual Language** (`docs/12`) · **Creative Concept — The Confident Host** (`docs/13`) · **North Star** (`docs/14`) · OS Hero System archetypes.

## The brief the Hero must answer
- **Blocking Question:** *"Can this one place actually host my thing — at my scale, near me — and is it easy to deal with?"* (now conference-led). *(BKB §D.)*
- **North Star to embody:** *"Every occasion — and everyone in it — held in capable, caring hands."*
- **The concept's job:** be the front door of **The Confident Host** and **close the gap** — make the online feeling match the on-arrival awe (the client's stated core problem). *(docs/13.)*
- **Hard rules:** capability **shown, not claimed**; **certified halal** is a headline welcome (never "friendly"); **no alcohol/bar** cues; never show non-sellable assets; primary action **"Book a viewing."** *(onboarding §13.)*
- **Client steer:** *"the D'Charmant Glass Hall at dusk is our strongest single image — treat it as the hero."* *(onboarding §11.)*
- **Two guests:** primary = a **procurement officer** (residential conference 150–500, wants proof/confidence/message-match); secondary = a **wedding couple** (wants beauty/warmth/belonging). The Hero must serve both without becoming two sites.

## Direction 1 — "The Glass Hall at Dusk" · Editorial Full-Bleed *(OS archetype A)*
- **Core idea:** one cinematic full-bleed frame of the D'Charmant Glass Hall at dusk — chandeliers lit, 360° garden — with copy bottom-anchored on a controlled scrim and a single primary CTA.
- **Emotional objective:** awe, "grander & warmer than I expected," *received*.
- **Concept fit:** the purest gap-close — the on-arrival feeling delivered in one image; the host's front door.
- **North Star fit:** the hall *held* for your occasion; H1 can state the promise directly.
- **Photography:** the client's named hero asset; dusk, glass, chandeliers, garden. Separate 4:5 mobile crop.
- **Layout philosophy:** image is the argument; everything else recedes; lower-third copy; one focal point.
- **Hierarchy:** image → H1 (promise) → one sub → **Book a viewing** → one trust chip.
- **Strengths:** highest premium ceiling; fastest emotional transfer; uses the strongest asset; pure slow-luxury.
- **Weaknesses:** carries little *information* — breadth, capability (1,300 hall), location and "one invoice" aren't explicit; OS warns editorial is weak for the *capability/conference* buyer; risk of "beautiful… but can it hold my 300 delegates?"
- **Best audience:** couples + leisure; under-serves procurement **unless** upgraded with a proof layer.
- **Paid / organic:** **both** for brand/emotional & Meta/wedding; weaker for paid *conference search* (no capability/location message-match) unless proof is added.

## Direction 2 — "One Site, One Team, One Invoice" · Split Copy / Evidence *(archetype B)*
- **Core idea:** left = the proposition in words (H1 + capability facts: 1,300-seat hall · 50 beds · certified halal · one invoice · Pretoria & Centurion); right = evidence (dusk glass hall, or a 3-image collage: hall + a real conference set + gardens). Primary + secondary CTA.
- **Emotional objective:** confidence / safe hands — "they can clearly handle this."
- **Concept fit:** capability **shown, not claimed**; the confident host proving competence.
- **North Star fit:** "held in capable hands" is literally the left column; "everyone in it" via the halal line.
- **Photography:** real evidence collage (Zaydtex-style layered proof) or the dusk hero on the right.
- **Layout philosophy:** asymmetric 7/5; copy-forward, information-carrying.
- **Hierarchy:** eyebrow (location) → H1 → capability strip → CTA → evidence.
- **Strengths:** the workhorse; balances information + proof; **best for the primary conference buyer** and for SEO copy volume; **excellent message-match for paid search** ("conference venue with accommodation Pretoria").
- **Weaknesses:** most "templated" if executed lazily; lower emotional wow than full-bleed; mobile needs a real restructure; splits the client's hero asset into a panel rather than a full-bleed statement.
- **Best audience:** conference/corporate (primary); also weddings.
- **Paid / organic:** **both** — strongest of all for paid search + organic depth.

## Direction 3 — "What Brings You?" · Editorial + Journey Selector *(A + F)*
- **Core idea:** full-bleed dusk estate image with a restrained 4-path selector — Weddings · Conferences · Celebrations · Stay — the host asking "what brings you?"; persistent Book a viewing.
- **Emotional objective:** welcome + self-recognition — "my thing is here."
- **Concept fit:** the gracious host reading the room; serves both audiences without diluting.
- **North Star fit:** *"every occasion"* made literal by the selector; "everyone in it" via a halal chip.
- **Photography:** dusk hero + optional small per-path thumbnails.
- **Layout philosophy:** full-bleed + a selector band (2×2 on mobile).
- **Hierarchy:** H1 (category + location) → selector → CTA/trust.
- **Strengths:** answers **breadth + "is it for me"** instantly; routes 4 audiences; ideal for a multi-service estate home.
- **Weaknesses:** the selector **dilutes the single emotional hit** and adds a second focal point; for *single-intent paid traffic* a selector raises attention-ratio (competing choices) versus a message-matched single path.
- **Best audience:** all — organic/home/brand entry.
- **Paid / organic:** **organic/brand** best; paid single-intent weaker (better landed on the specific hub). *Better demoted to a band directly below the Hero than used as the Hero device.*

## Direction 4 — "Everything This Estate Can Hold" · Progressive Capability reveal *(archetype H)*
- **Core idea:** sticky proposition column + a right column that reveals the **venue registry as capability cards** on scroll (Bastille 650 · D'Charmant 550 · SCC 1,300 · Martels Dungeon 130…), turning scroll into proof of scale.
- **Emotional objective:** awe at scale + capability — "there is far more here than I expected."
- **Concept fit:** capability *made visible*; the surprise-on-arrival delivered online — the client's core problem, answered structurally.
- **North Star fit:** literally a registry of *occasions it can hold*.
- **Photography:** dimmed dusk backdrop behind real venue cards.
- **Layout philosophy:** sticky/scroll, dense-but-rhythmic.
- **Hierarchy:** sticky H1 + proposition → scrolling capability cards → CTA.
- **Strengths:** uniquely differentiating; directly answers "can you hold my size?"; **surfaces the invisible SCC 1,300 + 3-site scale**; strong for conference buyers.
- **Weaknesses:** highest build complexity + **fragile sticky positioning**; can read technical/less warm (risk to the hospitality + elegance pillars); LCP/perf risk; over-indexes capability over emotion; not "restrained."
- **Best audience:** conference/corporate.
- **Paid / organic:** both, but heavy — an organic depth play. *Its mechanic is arguably better as a mid-page section on the Conferences page than as the Hero.*

## Direction 5 — "Received" · Type-Led Statement *(archetype G)*
- **Core idea:** no dominant photograph; the North Star promise set as a large, confident Fraunces statement + one small proof cluster (certified halal · 1,300-seat · Pretoria & Centurion) + one CTA.
- **Emotional objective:** quiet confidence, the host's calm; elegance foregrounded.
- **Concept fit:** the *manner* (elegance) foregrounded; specific-beats-grand.
- **North Star fit:** the promise **is** the Hero, in words.
- **Photography:** minimal/none — useful if imagery is thin (rooms/spa gaps).
- **Layout philosophy:** asymmetric type statement + proof cluster.
- **Hierarchy:** H1 statement → proof cluster → CTA.
- **Strengths:** fastest LCP; **zero photography dependency**; genuinely premium; ownable.
- **Weaknesses:** **no emotional transfer from imagery — which directly contradicts the concept's job (close the gap: the venues are more impressive than words).** Wastes the client's strongest asset; unforgiving; against the explicit client steer.
- **Best audience:** expert/organic.
- **Paid / organic:** organic/brand; poor for the emotional gap-close.

## Direction 6 — "The Welcome" · Hospitality-first Editorial *(archetype A, warmth)*
- **Core idea:** a full-bleed frame that leads with **warmth and belonging** — a beautifully set glass hall / a gracious moment of arrival at golden hour — with the **certified-halal welcome** surfaced early and a warm, promise-led H1.
- **Emotional objective:** welcome + belonging + cared-for (the hospitality pillar leading).
- **Concept fit:** the gracious host; belonging (the halal welcome) given pride of place.
- **North Star fit:** *"everyone in it… caring hands"* foregrounded.
- **Photography:** warm, human-touched estate detail (set hall, arrival) — real, no stock people.
- **Layout philosophy:** full-bleed, lower-third, warm.
- **Hierarchy:** image → warm H1 → halal/welcome trust → CTA.
- **Strengths:** differentiates from **cold corporate competitors**; leads with the **halal belonging edge** (a genuine differentiator); deeply on-brand for weddings + community/halal bookings.
- **Weaknesses:** warmth-first can **under-signal capability/scale** for the procurement buyer ("lovely — but can it hold 300?"); overlaps Direction 1.
- **Best audience:** weddings + community/halal + leisure.
- **Paid / organic:** both; Meta/wedding strong; weaker for paid conference search.

## Direction 7 — "Proof in the First Frame" · Capability / Trust-Stack *(archetypes C/D)*
- **Core idea:** an information-forward, substantive Hero that puts the decisive proof above the fold — a real image of a **hall set at scale**, H1 (proposition), a compact **capability + trust strip** (1,300-seat hall · 50 beds · certified halal · one invoice · Pretoria & Centurion · review volume), and Book a viewing. Density as a feature.
- **Emotional objective:** reassurance through substance — "these people are serious and can carry my event."
- **Concept fit:** capability shown to a skeptical procurement buyer — with the *Bbettr upgrade*: premium type + spacing on a substantive structure, so it stays elegant.
- **North Star fit:** "held in capable hands" via visible, specific proof.
- **Photography:** a real conference/banquet set at scale (a room ready and full).
- **Layout philosophy:** substantive but rhythmic; image + facts.
- **Hierarchy:** eyebrow → H1 → capability/trust strip → CTA.
- **Strengths:** **best message-match for the #1 revenue buyer** + paid search; answers capability + trust instantly; surfaces the invisible SCC scale + halal.
- **Weaknesses:** density risks losing the *quiet elegance* and the *emotion*; could read corporate if not art-directed with restraint; trades the dusk-glass-hall "wow" for a functional set shot.
- **Best audience:** conference/corporate (primary).
- **Paid / organic:** **both** — strong for paid search + organic conference.

## Elimination (and why)
- **✂ 5 "Received"** — its no-imagery premise contradicts the concept's central job (the venues are more impressive than words; the gap can't be closed with type). Keep only as an emergency fallback if photography truly fails.
- **✂ 4 "Everything It Holds"** — the *idea* is excellent but wrong as a Hero: fragile, complex, over-technical, low on warmth/elegance. **Reassign its mechanic to a mid-page capability section on the Conferences page** (proof, not front door).
- **✂ 3 "What Brings You"** — routing is valuable but dilutes the single emotional hit and hurts single-intent paid pages. **Demote the journey selector to a band directly beneath the Hero** — keep the emotion *and* the routing.
- **✂ 6 "The Welcome"** — its strength (the halal-belonging warmth) is real but it under-signals capability for the primary buyer; **fold its halal-welcome prominence into the recommended direction** rather than lead with warmth alone.

Left standing: **1, 2, 7** — the emotion end (1), the workhorse (2), the capability end (7).

## Recommendation

### ⭐ Primary — Direction 1 **upgraded with a proof layer** ("The Glass Hall at Dusk, Held")
Direction 1 executed per the OS rule *editorial **plus** evidence* (never minimalism without evidence): the **D'Charmant Glass Hall at dusk full-bleed** (the client's named asset — maximum gap-close and emotion) with **bottom-anchored, North-Star-led copy** and a **compact capability + trust strip absorbed from Direction 7** (1,300-seat hall · 50 beds · certified halal · one invoice · Pretoria & Centurion) + **Book a viewing**. The halal-welcome prominence from Direction 6 rides in the trust strip; the journey selector from Direction 3 sits as a band *directly below* the Hero.
**Why this is the one — it is everything we built, visible at once:**
- **Creative Concept:** capable *and* gracious *and* quietly elegant in a single frame — emotion (the image) with capability shown, not claimed (the proof strip). Closes the gap.
- **North Star:** the hall *held* for your occasion; the strip proves "capable hands"; the halal line proves "everyone in it."
- **BKB / Marketing:** honours the client's explicit hero steer; serves the couple (beauty) *and* the procurement buyer (proof); message-matchable for paid; Book-a-viewing primary.
- **Visual Language:** full-bleed dusk imagery, estate-green scrim, one champagne CTA, Fraunces H1, slow-luxury motion, a restrained glass motif — exactly `docs/12`.
- **Watch-outs to resolve at design:** the proof strip must not clutter the image (contrast measured over the dusk region); mobile 4:5 crop must keep the chandeliers + one proof line + CTA in the fold budget.

### ⭐ Backup — Direction 2 "One Site, One Team, One Invoice" (Split Copy / Evidence)
If, in design, the single full-bleed image can't carry enough capability for the procurement buyer — or for the **Conferences page hero specifically** (the #1 paid-search landing page) — the split gives maximum message-match: proposition + capability facts left, real evidence right. It is also the natural template for the Conferences hub even if Primary wins the Home hero.

### Note on scope
The **Home** Hero = Primary (brand + both audiences). Per-page heroes inherit the same DNA but shift emphasis — the **Conferences** hero leans toward the Backup (capability/message-match), a **Weddings** hero leans fuller-editorial/warmth. One system, emphasis by intent (`docs/07`).
