## 1. Photography philosophy

**The emotional role.** Photography is the single largest driver of how premium — and how *true* — Velmoré feels, and the main lever that **closes the gap** the client named: *"the online presence does not match the venues; people are visibly surprised on arrival."* Every image's job is to deliver, on screen, the feeling of walking onto the estate.

- **Supports the North Star** — *"Every occasion — and everyone in it — held in capable, caring hands."* Imagery shows the occasion *held*: a room set and lit and waiting, a team mid-preparation, guests received. It shows *everyone* included — the certified-halal welcome made visible (a beautifully set halal table, never alcohol).
- **Supports The Confident Host** — we photograph the *evidence of a host*: the room already prepared, the doors open, warm light spilling out. Capability is **shown, not claimed**; warmth is felt, not stated; elegance is the restraint of the frame.
- **The balance (every image leans, but the set covers all four):**
  - **Emotion** — dusk, warmth, arrival, the estate at its best hour (hero, weddings, gardens).
  - **Capability** — scale and readiness (the 1,300-seat hall set, conference configurations, breadth).
  - **Trust** — real, un-fakeable places and people; the certified-halal kitchen; real weddings.
  - **Proof** — specific, verifiable (a real venue at a real capacity, real rooms, real food).
- **Gap-close test (apply to every image):** *would a guest, on arrival, recognise this as more impressive than they expected — and as genuinely this place?* If it flatters falsely or underwhelms, it fails.

## 2. The photography language (one grade for the whole estate)

A single, consistent look so 200 images read as one estate. Applies to every frame unless a per-image note overrides.

| Dimension | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Colour grading** | Warm-natural, estate-true: **warm ivory/champagne highlights, deep estate greens, honeyed interior light**, cool-neutral shadows. No teal-orange, no trendy filters, no Instagram presets. |
| **Exposure** | Natural and slightly airy by day; protected highlights at dusk (never blow the chandeliers/glass). Never crushed blacks or flat/underexposed. |
| **Contrast** | Medium, filmic; soft highlight roll-off; retain shadow detail. Not punchy/HDR, not muddy. |
| **Editing style** | Editorial and restrained; true-to-life; minimal retouching; no compositing, no fake skies, no heavy vignettes, no over-sharpening. |
| **White balance** | Consistent across the set. Daylight ~5200–5600K; **warm ~3000–3200K for lit interiors at dusk** — embrace *warm-within-cool* on the glass hall. Never mixed/green casts. |
| **Dynamic range** | Preserve highlight **and** shadow detail; dusk frames hold warm interior + deep-blue exterior together. |
| **Sky treatment** | Real skies only; **dusk / golden hour / soft overcast** preferred; gentle, never oversaturated or dramatised. |
| **Interior lighting** | Warm practical light (chandeliers, lamps, candlelight-tone) — inviting and layered. Never flat overhead fluorescent; never harsh flash. |
| **Exterior lighting** | Golden hour or soft overcast; gardens lush and green. Avoid harsh midday sun and hard shadows. |
| **Seasonal consistency** | One season per gallery/set (gardens green and lush); never mix bare-winter with high-summer in the same set. |
| **Human styling** | Real guests/couples/delegates/staff — natural, at ease, never model-posed or stock. Diverse and respectful; **modest, appropriate styling** (important for the Muslim-community and halal-booking audience). |
| **Clothing** | Refined, timeless, estate-toned neutrals; smart corporate for conferences; elegant for weddings. No loud logos, no dated fashion, no competitor branding. |
| **Architectural emphasis** | Lead with the French-Provence identity: the **glass hall + chandeliers**, arches, Tuscan red brick (Bastille), the gardens. Let rectangles be rectangles (one radius, no blob/tilt). |
| **Hospitality moments** | The "received" feeling: a set halal table, doors open, a lit room, staff lighting candles, a couple arriving, a poured (non-alcoholic) welcome. |
| **Motion philosophy** | Slow, barely-noticed (per `12` §13): hero video muted, ≤8s, poster = the still, dusk light shifting imperceptibly; no fast cuts, no parallax on text. |

### What must NEVER appear (content rules — `BKB`/onboarding §13)
- **No alcohol / bar / cocktails / wine / drinks service** — anywhere. Martels Dungeon has a built-in bar as *architecture* → show the **space**, never the bar.
- **No non-sellable assets:** the 50 old-wing rooms, the **large pool** and **field** (under restoration), the small glass garden boardroom. *(The operational hotel pool area for leisure/welcome-drinks is fine; the "large pool" under restoration is not.)*
- **No stock people as subjects**, no cutout objects, no fabricated/AI "Velmoré" scenes presented as real.
- Nothing that undercuts the certified-halal welcome; no under-construction / messy / empty-cold spaces; no over-processed HDR; no dated fashion; no competitor branding.
