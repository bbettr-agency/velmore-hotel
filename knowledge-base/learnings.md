### Candidate OS learnings (for `LEARNINGS.md` after validation — do not promote yet)
- **Discovery must check for a parent group / multiple sites early** — a single-brand assumption hid a whole 1,300-pax venue here.
- **Never publish capacities/room-counts scraped from a client's own live site** — treat them as `[assumed]` until the client confirms a registry; the live site was materially wrong.
