## C. Market Position

- **Position:** the **all-in-one celebration & conference estate between Pretoria and Johannesburg** — hosts the whole occasion (marry · meet · celebrate · dine · unwind · stay) for **8 to 1,500 guests** on one estate. `[verified capability]`
- **Genuine advantages (verifiable):** (1) all-in-one **at scale** (no local rival matches stay + large weddings + conferencing + spa on one estate); (2) **literal midway location**; (3) distinctive **French-Provence riverside estate**; (4) **residential conferencing** (conference + on-site room block). `[verified]`
- **Biggest strengths (from reviews):** gardens/setting, river & mountain views, spacious rooms (post-renovation), the spa. `[verified — review themes]`
- **Biggest weaknesses (candid):** moderate **Google 4.0★**; recurring review complaints on **event catering (cold/late food at large functions), cleanliness/maintenance, service consistency** during "management transitions"; a **dated, fragmented web presence** (two domains, demo content live). These are operational + brand risks the site cannot hide. `[verified — review themes + audit]`
- **Why customers choose Velmore:** scale + one-place convenience + the estate's look + location. **Why they choose competitors:** boutique intimacy (Kleinkaap), stronger single-vertical focus (Casa Toscana weddings), brand trust (Protea/Marriott), or polished sites. `[assumed from competitor analysis]`
- **Competitor set (intelligence):** Royal Elephant (same river, **broken domain**), Irene Country Lodge/Hotel (prestige, **mid-rebrand**), Kleinkaap (best site, **only 34 suites**), Casa Toscana, ANEW Centurion, Protea by Marriott Centurion, Lethabo Estate. Full teardown + gap map in `../docs/02-REDESIGN-BLUEPRINT.md` Part 4. **The wedge:** publish what the market hides — **capacities + package pricing** — while two rivals are disrupted. `[verified]`

## D. Customer

Four segments (detail + journeys in `../docs/02-REDESIGN-BLUEPRINT.md` Part 3):
- **A Leisure/staycation guest** — weekend getaway / spa break; comparison-shops OTAs.
- **B Couple / wedding planner** — building a venue shortlist; emotional + budget-anxious.
- **C Corporate / conference organiser** — sourcing on capacity + accommodation + location.
- **D Private-event client** — matric farewell, milestone birthday, gala, memorial; seasonal.

**Ranked objections (client-level — the psychology; on-page answers are project-level in the blueprint):**
1. "Can you actually hold *my* size?" (capacity fit)
2. "What will it cost?" (market-wide price opacity)
3. "Is the food / service actually good?" (real review complaint theme)
4. "Where exactly is it / how far from me?" (location ambiguity)
5. "Can I trust this place?" (moderate rating + dated site)

**Emotional arc:** arrives comparison-weary/unsure → must leave confident this is the capable, beautiful, easy choice.
